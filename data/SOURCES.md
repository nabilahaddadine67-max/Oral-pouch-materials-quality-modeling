# Source and provenance notes

## 1. Scientific Reports paper (physical and moisture properties)

Platt, S. P. et al., *Dissolution and physical characterization of oral nicotine pouch products*. Scientific Reports **16**, 4406 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34556-5

- `physical_properties_table6.csv` — transcribed from Table 6 of the peer-reviewed paper (also checked against the user-supplied screenshot). Values including SDs are *published summary measurements*, not individual replicates.
- `moisture_table8.csv` — reported product-level oven-volatiles and water activity values; distinguish oven volatiles from chemical-specific water measurements.
- `physical_properties_with_calculations.csv` and `physical_moisture_combined.csv` — generated by the analysis script.
- The paper is open access. Check the published license and provide attribution to the original authors when reusing data. No image or figure is copied here.

## 2. Public patent application (formulation/release)

*Non-tobacco oral nicotine pouch composition*. US20260232007A1, published Aug 13 2026. https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260232007A1/en

- `patent_table22_release.csv` — transcribed from Example 6B, Table 22. Each sample's premix identity, presence/absence of sodium alginate, presence/absence of CaCl2, and release at 2, 5, 10, and 30 min are in the original document.
- Table 21 provides composition context. Alginate is present at 2 wt% when selected; CaCl2 at 1 wt% when selected. Premix II/VI are distinct ingredient blends; they should be coded as a two-level categorical factor, not interpreted as a pure change in nicotine concentration.
- Formulation composition varies additionally in xylitol, purified water, and NaCl (C19/C20). The apparent 2^3 structure supports DESCRIPTIVE comparisons but not clean attribution of effects exclusively to three ingredients.
- Public disclosure is not a license to practice patented technology. This repository is independent educational/public-literature analysis, not manufacturing instructions or commercialization advice.

## Data handling

All values are manually transcribed from public sources and may reflect rounding. Cross-check against the originals before citing numerical inferences. No proprietary, employer, or personal laboratory data are used. Full underlying replicate-level data for the paper are not provided here.
