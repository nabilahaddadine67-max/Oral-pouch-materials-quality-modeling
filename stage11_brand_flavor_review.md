# Stage 11 — Brand-specific and flavor-specific evidence expansion (November 2026)

## Scope and provenance

This is a **targeted, non-exhaustive extension** of Stage 10. Entries are sourced from peer-reviewed journal articles and one clearly labeled published patent. It does **not** assert that every publication worldwide has been captured. The supporting CSV contains **14 source-grounded evidence entries**; each row specifies exactly what product/variant was studied and a direct paper link. It contains selected published numerical results rather than full copyrighted data tables. No proprietary employer datasets were used.

## Main new evidence

1. **on! PLUS is not interchangeable with on!** The on! PLUS 2025 *ACS Omega* study examines nine flavor/strength combinations (Mint, Wintergreen, Tobacco × 6, 9, 12 mg) across three production lots over 12 months at 25 ± 2°C and 60 ± 5% RH. Nicotine N′-oxide increased from 0.14–0.42% at baseline to 0.75–1.32% at month 12 (percent relative to targeted nicotine content). This is chemical-stability evidence, not total flavor-retention evidence. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.5c00263).
2. **on! base brand** was separately evaluated by Raman spectroscopy in five flavors (Original, Mint, Wintergreen, Citrus, Berry) and labeled 2/4/8 mg strengths. Chemometric flavor differentiation was demonstrated; the study does not establish precise concentration of each aroma compound. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.5c06748).
3. **ZYN coolant chemistry:** In a 2026 comparison, Chill and Classic showed WS-3 about 0.24 ± 0.02 mg per pouch, whereas Smooth and Original lacked measured WS-3 and menthol. Marketing descriptors are therefore not a reliable stand-in for analytical chemical identity. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1136/tc-2025-059940).
4. **Flavor-specific quantitation:** A market-sampling study reported menthol of 15.1 ± 1.2 mg/pouch for Rogue Peppermint, methyl salicylate of 14.9 ± 0.5 mg/pouch for Rogue Wintergreen, and triacetin of 29.0 ± 0.1 mg/pouch for Juice Head Raspberry Lemonade Mint 12 mg. These are **selected products**, not intrinsic brand averages. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1093/ntr/ntaf105).
5. **Sweeteners also matter:** Previously analyzed on!, ZYN, and VELO products contained measured acesulfame-K in a subset of samples, including nominally unflavored varieties, while original VELO lines reported sucralose. The paper's market samples were purchased in 2020–2022; do not assume today's recipes are identical. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10849646/).
6. **Performance depends on the test:** In a 2025 experimental study, extracts from ZYN Cool Mint, Dryft Spearmint, and VELO Mint yielded apparent porcine-buccal permeability coefficients of 38.24, 31.06, and 14.84 ×10⁻⁶ cm/s, respectively, using defined artificial-saliva conditions. Different brand/strength/pH combinations prevent assigning a causal flavor effect. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.5c06007).
7. **Flavor retention in actual pouch materials:** A published patent has week-resolved measurements for limonene and linalyl acetate. After 3 weeks at 30°C/75% RH, applicant-reported retention was 69% and 95% for a 5% coconut-fat matrix versus 3% and 5% without added oil. Fat loading co-varied with moisture; a causal conclusion is not justified. [Source](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11844368B2/en).
8. **Additional comprehensive chemistry:** A 2025 analytical study covered 30 commercial pouches from 14 brands, including nicotine characteristics, water, pH, humectants, organic acids and cooling agents. Preserve product-level identity and assay method before joining with any other dataset. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.microc.2025.116196).

## Cross-study synthesis

The literature suggests at least four partially separable axes: (a) filler microstructure and water mobility; (b) identity and measured load of flavor/cooling/sweetener compounds; (c) packaging and matrix-related chemical stability; and (d) test-medium-dependent dissolution and transport. The evidence is insufficient to assign one *overall brand quality ranking*, including for on! PLUS, without common specifications, matched measurements and independently validated weights.

**Important separation:** In vitro permeability, nicotine release, flavor retention, and chemical impurities are distinct endpoints; higher values do not uniformly mean higher quality or better safety.

## Research gaps sharpened by the new evidence

- Chemical flavor identity is not reliably determined by the marketing flavor name, as shown by WS-3 in ZYN Classic/Chill.
- Few public datasets provide **the same SKU × lot × flavor × humidity × time** measured jointly for aroma concentration, degradation, moisture, and filler microstructure.
- Sweetener and coolant profiles change by line and strength and may be confused with nominal flavor comparisons.
- Published studies compare different dose levels, pH, matrices, and test media; factorial causal estimates require matched and controlled conditions.
- No published entries here include pore-accessible and same-range total MCC pore volume for a validated PAI.

## Proposed experimental program (see CSV)

**X01 — Brand/flavor analytical map:** Document specific variants and lots; assess flavor markers, coolants and sweeteners, water and impurities with appropriate method validation. Obtain and assess necessary permissions for regulated materials.

**X02 — Flavor label versus chemical inventory:** Target menthol, methyl salicylate, WS-3/WS-23, select sweeteners, and suitable flavor markers across selected product variants. Test blanks and extraction recovery; distinguish detectable from absent.

**X03 — Controlled nicotine-free flavor × cellulose DOE:** Cross MCC grade, HPC, RH and flavor identity; measure moisture sorption, retention, physical cohesion and chemical change. This can establish material structure–property relationships but cannot itself predict nicotine exposure.

**X04 — Fabric/filler separation:** Independently vary pouch fabric while holding filler constant; test wicking, mechanical integrity and hydration to separate fabric porosity from MCC behavior.

**X05 — Prospective scoring model validation:** Use matched samples measured across all quality domains, report missingness and uncertainty, and hold out full lots for validation before proposing an overall PQPS.

## Publication practice and reproducibility

- Distinguish peer-reviewed results from patent disclosures and distinguish measurements from our derived results.
- Store source URLs, years, model/brand, flavor/strength and assay medium; retain exact reference-table provenance for future raw extraction.
- Do not combine chemical measurements from separate product lines into a fictional formulation.
- Do not imply manufacturer endorsement, independent verification of patent measurements, or a validated commercial optimum.
- If adapting tables or figures, check each journal's reuse conditions and attribute accordingly. This addendum paraphrases selected findings and independently organizes them, without reproducing full figures.
