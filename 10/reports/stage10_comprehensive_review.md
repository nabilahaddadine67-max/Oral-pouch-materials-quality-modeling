# Stage 10 — Comprehensive evidence map and research-gap roadmap (2026)

**Scope:** Structured, non-exhaustive scoping review of peer-reviewed oral-nicotine-pouch materials, formulation, analytical, transport, stability, safety, and relevant public patents available by 22 November 2026. It is **not** a PRISMA systematic review. Public source data and proposed experiments are clearly separated.

**Purpose:** Identify what is known, what is not measured on matched formulations, and which independent **nicotine-free** materials-science experiments could test the missing mechanisms. No claim of optimized nicotine delivery, commercial safety, or validated product quality is made.

## Executive synthesis

1. Published product comparisons show substantial variation in physical/chemical properties, but cross-brand correlations are not causal formulation effects.
2. Moisture content, water activity, water mobility, and pore structure are distinct: neither oven volatiles nor tapped-bed void fraction measures internal water-accessible MCC pores.
3. Release and permeation depend on protocol and media composition. Comparisons require matched test conditions.
4. Patents provide valuable factorial and retention tables, but several ingredients co-vary and independent replication is often missing.
5. Flavor retention must be measured compound-by-compound with validated analytical recovery and transformation-product monitoring.
6. on! and on! PLUS are different products; only the latter appears in the identified 12-month degradant stability study.
7. No published source in the register supports assigning a measured PAI or complete six-domain PQPS to all seven commercial products.

## Pore Accessibility Index: measurement gate

PAI = 100 × (water-accessible pore volume / compatible total pore volume). Denominator and numerator must refer to the same pore-size range, hydration state, and material unit. DSC freezable water, DVS uptake, and density-derived bulk-bed porosity are not interchangeable pore volumes. Until independently validated, report these separately rather than produce a brand ranking.

## Experimental priority

**First:** E1 (MCC pore-water measurement) and E2 (MCC/HPC/humidity factorial). **Second:** E3 (nicotine-free flavor/humidity), E4 (nonwoven wetting and integrity). **Third:** E5–E7 validation, multiresponse model and external testing. All study matrices are *proposals*, not measured data.

## Screening method and limits

Targeted searches on 22 Nov 2026 combined oral nicotine pouch / nicotine pouch / pouch filler with characterization, cellulose, MCC, hygroscopicity, dynamic vapor sorption, nicotine release, permeation, flavoring, stability, thermal analysis, toxicant, and packaging. Sources were screened for relevance to scientific/analytical mechanisms, 2023–2026 preferred but older foundational sources retained, and patents kept separate from peer review. No exhaustive database export, duplicate-removal count, risk-of-bias instrument, formal PRISMA flow, or meta-analysis was undertaken.

**Ethics/quality:** Public patent data can be cited, but published claims are not independent verification and patents may still carry commercial rights. Clinical evidence is summarized for research context, not used to create consumer-optimization recommendations.
